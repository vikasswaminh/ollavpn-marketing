● THE HONEST PART

Three things to know up front.

● IN OFFICE

Five days a week, in person.

No remote. No hybrid. No "flexible Fridays." We answer phones during business hours and we work shoulder to shoulder. If a fully-remote role is what you want, this is not it — and we'd rather say that here than after your interview.

● ONE CAMPUS

Bengaluru. HSR Layout.

One office, one team, one timezone. No satellite locations, no hub-and-spoke. If commuting to HSR Layout isn't workable for you, this job isn't workable for you. The campus is on Sector 6, walking distance from the BWSSB junction.

● EARLY STAGE

Year zero to year one.

We're scaling from a team to a company. Your first twelve months will look different from your last twelve. Process gets written as we go. If you want a job that's already figured out, this isn't it. If you want to figure things out, it is.

Sales Executive

BENGALURU · FULL TIME · IN OFFICE · MON–SAT
10 SEATS

Take OllaVPN to SMBs and IT buyers across India. Outbound prospecting, inbound qualification, demo, close. You'll own the funnel from first touch to signature.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

  • Run outbound campaigns targeting IT decision-makers at Indian SMBs and mid-market firms
  • Qualify inbound leads from the website, partner channels, and word of mouth
  • Run live product demos, in-person where possible, over video otherwise

  • Keep CRM hygiene tight — every conversation logged, every deal forecasted

  • Comfortable making the first cold call of the day before reading your email
  • Working English and Hindi; other Indian languages are an asset, not a requirement
  • Curiosity about how the internet actually works — you don't need to know already
  • Track record of hitting quota, or — if early-career — a clear story of how you'd start

BONUS POINTS

  • Experience selling to compliance-sensitive industries (BFSI, healthcare, legal)
  • Background in networking, VPN, or endpoint security sales

Network Support Engineer

BENGALURU · FULL TIME · IN OFFICE · MON–SAT · SHIFT-BASED
20 SEATS

Frontline for paying customers and free-tier user reports. Read the trace, reproduce the issue, escalate to engineering when warranted. Most days you'll close half a dozen tickets and write up a pattern that saves the next ten.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

  • Triage inbound user reports from in-app help, email, and phone
  • Reproduce reported issues against a real client build on Windows, macOS, and (soon) mobile
  • Read packet captures, route tables, DNS resolver state — and tell us in plain English what they say
  • Maintain the runbook: every recurring issue gets a documented fix, every fix gets a regression note
  • Escalate to engineering with a clean reproducer when the trail leaves user-fixable territory
  • Cover one of two shifts: 9 AM–6 PM IST or 2 PM–11 PM IST, six days a week, rotation by mutual agreement

WHAT YOU BRING

  • Solid TCP/IP fundamentals: addressing, routing, DNS, NAT, MTU, firewall basics
  • Comfort on the command line: tcpdump or Wireshark, traceroute, dig, ping, nslookup
  • CCNA-level networking knowledge OR equivalent hands-on experience — certification not required
  • Patient writing voice — every closed ticket leaves the user feeling heard, not handled
  • Working English; Hindi or another Indian language is helpful for phone support

BONUS POINTS

  • Prior experience supporting a VPN, firewall, or remote-access product
  • Familiarity with WireGuard, OpenVPN, or IPsec at the protocol level
  • Some Linux scripting (bash, Python) to automate the boring parts of your day

● HOW TO APPLY

One inbox.

Send four things.

  1. Email careers@ollavpn.com
  2. Subject: <Role> · <Your Name> — exactly
  3. Resume as PDF, under 2 MB
  4. A two-line note: why this role, why Bengaluru

What happens next.

  1. First reply within 5 business days — even when it's a no
  2. Phone screen with the hiring lead (30 minutes)
  3. On-site round at the HSR Layout campus (half day)
  4. Offer or honest no within the same week

We read every email. If we say no, we'll tell you why. If we say yes, the offer comes with a clear comp letter — base, variable (where applicable), and standard statutory benefits. Compensation is market-aligned for Bengaluru; we share the actual number at the offer stage, after we've understood the level you're operating at.
