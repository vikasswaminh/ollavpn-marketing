In-Tunnel DNS: Why It's Crucial for Your VPN Privacy

You're probably using a VPN to keep your online activity private, but have you ever thought about how your device finds the websites you want to visit? That's DNS, and if your VPN isn't handling it correctly, your browsing habits might still be exposed, even when you think you're fully protected. Many VPNs overlook this common pitfall, but OllaVPN's in-tunnel DNS makes sure your DNS requests are just as secure and private as your traffic, closing a critical privacy gap.

Unlike weak VPNs that fall back to public DNS resolvers or leak queries at the OS level, OllaVPN enforces kernel-level firewall rules blocking port-53 leaks and pairs every DNS query with post-quantum WireGuard encryption across all platforms on the free tier.

What is in-tunnel DNS and why is it so important?

QUICK ANSWER

In-tunnel DNS ensures that every domain lookup your device makes is encrypted and routed exclusively through the VPN tunnel to private, zero-log resolvers, completely shielding your destination requests from your ISP.

When you type a website address into your browser, your computer first needs to look up that domain's IP address. Without in-tunnel DNS, those lookup requests (DNS queries) travel unencrypted across your local network directly to your internet service provider (ISP). Even if your subsequent website traffic is encrypted, your ISP still captures a complete record of every single domain you attempt to visit.

In-tunnel DNS closes this critical security gap by hijacking your device's DNS settings at the network stack level. All DNS packets are encrypted and transported inside the secure WireGuard tunnel to private resolvers operated exclusively by OllaVPN.

How does OllaVPN's in-tunnel DNS work, step-by-step?

QUICK ANSWER

OllaVPN intercepts your DNS requests on your device, encrypts them, and sends them through the secure VPN tunnel to our private, no-log DNS resolvers, which then fetch the IP addresses for the websites you want to visit.

When you connect to OllaVPN, our app takes over your device's DNS settings. This means that instead of sending your domain name requests (like "ollavpn.com") to your internet provider's DNS servers, all those requests are immediately redirected to OllaVPN. From the moment you hit "connect," every bit of traffic, including your DNS queries, travels through our encrypted tunnel, making it impossible for your ISP or anyone else to see what sites you're trying to reach.

Once inside the tunnel, your DNS queries are sent directly to OllaVPN's private DNS resolvers. These aren't just any public DNS servers; they're infrastructure we operate ourselves, specifically designed to uphold our strict no-logs policy. They don't keep records of your activity, ensuring your browsing habits remain private. Using our own resolvers, rather than relying on third parties, is a critical part of how we protect your privacy end-to-end. Your requests stay within our trusted network until the very last step.

After our resolvers look up the IP address for the website you're visiting, that information is sent back to your device, still protected within the secure tunnel. Only then does your device know where to send the actual request for the website's content. This whole process happens incredibly fast, thanks to the efficiency of the WireGuard protocol that OllaVPN uses.

What do other VPNs often get wrong with DNS, and how is OllaVPN different?

QUICK ANSWER

Many VPNs leave your DNS requests vulnerable to leaks or use third-party servers, while OllaVPN encrypts and routes all your DNS traffic directly through its own secure, private resolvers inside the VPN tunnel.

A lot of VPNs, especially ad-funded or freemium throttled providers, overlook DNS security or cut corners. Even if your web traffic is encrypted, your DNS queries might still go through your ISP or a public third-party resolver. This creates a severe privacy hole known as a DNS leak.

Some VPNs try to fix this by using publicly available DNS resolvers like Cloudflare or Google. While these are faster than ISP resolvers, they are still third parties that log and analyze connection telemetry. Furthermore, weak clients fail to enforce firewall rules, causing the operating system to fall back to unencrypted local DNS.

OllaVPN operates its own private, zero-log Unbound DNS resolvers co-located on every exit node. DNS requests are resolved entirely in-memory with zero disk logging, eliminating DNS hijacking and accidental leaks.

What are the limitations of in-tunnel DNS, and what doesn't it solve?

QUICK ANSWER

In-tunnel DNS protects domain resolution and prevents ISP eavesdropping, but it does not stop browser fingerprinting, cookies, or phishing links you voluntarily click.

What It Solves: Encrypts all DNS queries, stops ISP browsing logs, blocks rogue Wi-Fi spoofing, and bypasses DNS-based website blocking.

What It Does Not Solve: Application-level trackers, tracking cookies stored in your browser, social media account logins, or malware downloads.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does in-tunnel DNS protect me against in plain terms? +

It stops your internet service provider (ISP) and anyone on your local network from seeing which websites you visit. All domain lookup requests are sealed and encrypted inside the VPN tunnel, ensuring complete destination privacy.

Can I customize my DNS servers in OllaVPN? +

Yes. While OllaVPN provides private, zero-log resolvers by default, you can easily configure custom primary and secondary DNS server IPs in the app settings, and they will still be routed securely through the encrypted tunnel.

Does in-tunnel DNS slow down my internet connection? +

No. Because our private DNS resolvers are co-located directly on each VPN server, lookups resolve in sub-milliseconds with local RAM caching, often outperforming congested ISP resolvers.

How does in-tunnel DNS prevent DNS hijacking on public Wi-Fi? +

On untrusted public Wi-Fi, rogue routers often intercept DNS requests to redirect users to malicious clones of banking and login pages. In-tunnel DNS bypasses local Wi-Fi DNS completely by encrypting queries directly to our verified servers.

Does in-tunnel DNS protect against cookies and browser fingerprinting? +

No. In-tunnel DNS secures your network-level destination lookups. For application-level privacy against tracking cookies and browser fingerprinting, we recommend using content blockers such as uBlock Origin alongside your VPN.

Does OllaVPN's resolver support DNSSEC validation? +

Yes. Our Unbound resolvers validate upstream DNSSEC cryptographic signatures from root and TLD authorities, rejecting forged or invalid records automatically.

How does in-tunnel DNS interact with IPv6? +

DNS lookups inside the tunnel support AAAA (IPv6) queries seamlessly. To prevent IPv6 leak vulnerabilities on unsupported networks, IPv6 traffic is safely disabled at the tunnel interface while apps connect cleanly via IPv4.

Yes. Every OllaVPN user on the $0 forever plan receives full in-tunnel DNS protection with no bandwidth caps on DNS, no card required, and zero activity logs.
