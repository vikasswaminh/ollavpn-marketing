Why two-person matters here

Most VPN companies have hundreds of employees, dozens of subcontractors, and a marketing team that doesn't know what their engineers ship. The bigger that team, the harder it is to make honest claims about what the product actually does — because nobody on staff has read all the code.

OllaVPN is two people. We've both read every line of the data-plane. When a blog post says “peer isolation is verified end-to-end,” we can point at the four sysctl + nftables + WireGuard config + automated e2e test that enforces it. When something breaks, one of us pages the other on Signal. The product is small enough that we can still tell the truth about it.

When that stops being true, we'll say so.
