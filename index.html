● WHY POST-QUANTUM, WHY NOW

A PQC VPN: post-quantum encryption, on by default.

Encryption you trust today is being recorded. "Harvest now, decrypt later" is real: encrypted traffic is stored now, to be broken once quantum computers are strong enough. A post-quantum VPN adds a quantum-resistant key exchange on top of today's encryption, so recorded traffic stays private. OllaVPN uses ML-KEM, the standard selected by NIST, alongside WireGuard on every connection. How our PQC VPN works →

Free VPN questions

Is OllaVPN really free?

Yes. OllaVPN is free with no card and no trial. The free plan includes up to 100 Mbps and 20 GB of data a month on one device.

Is there a free VPN for Mac?

Yes. OllaVPN has a free app for Mac (macOS 12 or later, Apple silicon and Intel). It is in beta: the first time, right-click the installer and choose Open.

Is there an OllaVPN app for iPhone or iPad?

Not yet. The iPhone and iPad app is on our roadmap and coming soon. Email us and we will tell you when it is ready.

What is a PQC VPN?

A PQC VPN adds post-quantum cryptography to the connection, so traffic recorded today cannot be decrypted later by a quantum computer. OllaVPN uses ML-KEM, the key-exchange standard selected by NIST.

Does OllaVPN keep logs?

OllaVPN does not log the sites you visit. It keeps sign-in records and daily data totals, which are needed to run the free allowance.

What happens after 20 GB?

The VPN keeps working at 1 Mbps until the monthly reset on the 1st (UTC). There is also a 5 GB daily limit, which resets at midnight UTC.
