Fair Use Policy

Effective 24 November 2026 · Part of our Terms of Service.

OllaVPN is 100% free, with no paid plan and no data charge. To keep it fast and usable for everyone, each account gets the allowance below. Almost everyone stays far inside it: on a typical day, half of our users move less than 50 MB.

Your allowance

  • One device per account at a time. Connecting a new device replaces the old one.
  • Speed: up to 100 Mbps download and up to 20 Mbps upload per device.
  • Daily data: full speed for your first 20 GB each day. After that, your speed drops to 1 Mbps. At 50 GB the connection stops until the next day. The day resets at 00:00 UTC.
  • Connections: up to 100 new connections per day. Reconnecting after a network change or switching location counts as one; normal use is well under 30.

What is not allowed

  • P2P file sharing, such as BitTorrent. Public torrent trackers are blocked on our network.
  • Sending email through mail servers from the tunnel. Outgoing mail ports (25, 465, 587) are blocked to stop spam. Webmail in your browser works normally; a desktop or phone mail app can still receive, but cannot send while you are connected.
  • Automated or scripted use, including unofficial or modified clients. Use the OllaVPN apps.
  • Getting around the limits with several accounts, temporary email addresses, or by sharing or reselling an account.
  • Anything listed under Acceptable use in our terms, such as attacks, scanning, spam, malware or fraud.

How we enforce it without looking at your traffic

We never inspect what you browse or download. The limits are enforced with counters on our servers: how many bytes and connections each device uses, nothing about what they contain. P2P is limited by blocking the addresses of public torrent trackers and by the upload cap, not by reading your traffic. Our Privacy Policy explains what we keep and for how long.

What happens if a limit is crossed

  1. Automatic slow-down or pause. Going past the daily data allowance slows or pauses your connection until the next day. Nothing else happens; this is the normal way the allowance works.
  2. Temporary suspension. Repeated or deliberate breaches of this policy can lead to a suspension, usually 24 hours. The message you see when signing in says when it ends, and it lifts on its own.
  3. Account closure. Serious abuse, such as attacks, spam or automated harvesting of our service, or continued breaches after a suspension, can lead to the account being disabled.

Questions and appeals

If you think we got it wrong, write to support@ollavpn.com from the email on your account and we will review it. To report abuse coming from our network, write to abuse@ollavpn.com.
