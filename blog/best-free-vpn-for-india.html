1. What does "really free" actually mean for a VPN in India?

QUICK ANSWER "Really free" means you can download, install, and use the VPN indefinitely without ever providing payment details or being auto-enrolled in a paid subscription — and "no logs" means the provider doesn't retain records of your browsing activity, connection timestamps, or IP address.

"No credit card" should mean exactly that — no card on file at all, not a card charged ₹0 today that silently begins billing after a short trial window. This pattern shows up constantly on app stores: a "free" download that actually initiates a trial auto-renewal trap.

"No logs" means the provider doesn't store data that could reconstruct your browsing activity — no record of sites visited, no timestamped connection logs tied to your identity, and no retained traffic history. A meaningful policy names exactly what isn't collected.

3. Why does India specifically need careful VPN vetting?

QUICK ANSWER India is one of the world's largest mobile markets, subject to CERT-In's 2022 data-retention directives and high concentrations of ad-monetized clone apps in app stores.

CERT-In's 2022 Data-Retention Directions: India's Computer Emergency Response Team required VPN providers with physical servers inside India to log customer identities, IP addresses, and connection timestamps. In response, privacy-first providers withdrew physical hardware from Indian soil, deploying virtual India-location servers hosted in privacy-friendly jurisdictions that still deliver Indian IP addresses without compromising no-logs guarantees.
