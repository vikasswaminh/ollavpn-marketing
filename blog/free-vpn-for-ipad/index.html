Quick Answer

An honest free VPN provides genuine encryption and an audited no-logs policy without monthly data limits, using a transparent business model (like an optional paid upgrade) rather than monetizing your personal data or bombarding you with ads.

6. Why Is a 10 Mbps Cap Enough for Most People on iPad?

Speed tests often encourage users to chase hundreds of megabits, but real-world iPad activities require surprisingly modest bandwidth:

  • 1080p HD video streaming: Requires 5 Mbps of sustained bandwidth.
  • High-definition video calls (Zoom, FaceTime): Requires 3 to 4 Mbps.
  • Web browsing and digital reading: Requires 1 to 2 Mbps.
  • Podcast and music streaming: Requires less than 0.5 Mbps.

At 10 Mbps, your daily iPad activities run smoothly without buffering. The only scenario where higher speeds matter is downloading massive 4K movie files or multi-gigabyte game updates.
